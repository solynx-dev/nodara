# Nodara Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026

Nodara is a privacy-focused, self-hosted family location-sharing application designed to give individuals and families control over their own data.

Unlike traditional location-sharing services, Nodara is designed to operate on infrastructure controlled by its users. We believe that your location, personal information, and private communications should remain yours.

This Privacy Policy explains how Nodara handles information when you use the Nodara iOS application, its associated software, and any optional services provided by the Nodara developer.

## 1. Who We Are

Nodara is an open-source, self-hosted application available through GitHub.

For the purposes of this policy, "Nodara," "we," "our," and "us" refer to the developer and maintainer of the Nodara project.

Nodara is not a centralized location-sharing service. Users deploy and operate their own servers and connect their devices to infrastructure they control.

**The Nodara developer does not operate a centralized server that stores or processes users' location histories or personal information.**

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

When using Nodara in its standard, self-hosted configuration, the Nodara developer does not collect, receive, access, or store:

- Real-time or historical location information.
- GPS coordinates, location histories, or movement patterns.
- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifiers.
- Family relationships, group memberships, or contact lists.
- Private messages or communications.
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
- User accounts, passwords, or authentication credentials.
- Information stored on your self-hosted server.
- Personal information generated through your use of Nodara.

Your location information is transmitted between your devices and your own server infrastructure.

We do not have access to this information simply because you use Nodara.

## 3. Self-Hosted Infrastructure and Data Ownership

Nodara is designed to allow users to operate their own infrastructure.

When you deploy Nodara on your own server, you are responsible for the infrastructure on which your information is stored and processed.

This may include:

- A home server or personal computer.
- A virtual private server (VPS).
- A privately managed cloud server.
- Other infrastructure under your control.

Your location data and other application information are processed by your own server and are not transmitted to the Nodara developer as part of normal application functionality.

The security, availability, backup, and retention of information on your server are your responsibility.

Your hosting provider may independently process information such as IP addresses, server access logs, or other technical information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

## 4. Optional Anonymous Usage Analytics

Nodara may offer an optional usage analytics feature to help the developer understand how widely the project is being used and improve the software.

**Analytics are disabled by default.**

No analytics information is transmitted to the Nodara developer unless a user or self-hoster explicitly opts in.

If enabled, analytics may include limited, anonymous information such as:

- Approximate installation or deployment counts.
- Aggregate active installation counts.
- Nodara software version.
- General usage statistics necessary to understand adoption of the project.

Analytics will not include:

- GPS coordinates or location histories.
- Names, email addresses, or other personal information.
- Family member identities or relationships.
- Private messages or communications.
- Authentication credentials.
- Device advertising identifiers.
- Information from users' self-hosted databases.

Analytics are intended to measure the adoption and general usage of Nodara, not to monitor individuals or their families.

We do not use analytics to track individuals across applications, websites, or services.

### Your Choice

You may choose whether to enable analytics.

- Analytics are off by default.
- Enabling analytics requires an explicit opt-in.
- You may disable analytics at any time.
- Disabling analytics stops future analytics transmissions.
- Your decision does not affect the core functionality of Nodara.

If you disable analytics, Nodara will not continue sending optional analytics information.

Any previously received anonymous analytics may be retained in aggregate form for the purpose of understanding project adoption. Where individual records exist, they will be deleted or de-identified when no longer necessary.

## 5. Location Permissions

Nodara may request access to your device's location services to provide location-sharing functionality.

Location permissions are controlled through your device's operating system.

Depending on the features you use, Nodara may request:

- Precise location access.
- Background location access.
- Permission to access location while using the application.

Location information is used to provide the functionality you choose to enable.

Your location data is transmitted to the server you have configured, not to a centralized Nodara server.

You may revoke location permissions through your device settings. Doing so may affect features that depend on location access.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Nodara is open-source software and may be deployed alongside third-party software or services chosen by the user.

Examples may include:

- Server hosting providers.
- Reverse proxies.
- DNS providers.
- Mapping services.
- Apple operating system services.
- Third-party integrations configured by the self-hoster.

These services may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

The Nodara developer does not control independently operated third-party infrastructure.

Where Nodara uses a third-party service directly, its data-handling practices will be disclosed as applicable.

## 7. Apple and iOS Services

The Nodara iOS application operates within Apple's iOS ecosystem.

Apple may independently collect information through operating system features, App Store services, crash reporting, or analytics services according to Apple's own privacy policies and your device settings.

The Nodara developer does not receive Apple's independently collected information unless Apple makes it available to us through a service we have chosen to use.

Any such information is separate from the location data stored on your own infrastructure.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Nodara does not maintain a centralized database of user location information or personal information.

As a result, the Nodara developer cannot delete information stored on a user's self-hosted server.

Users and self-hosters control the retention and deletion of their own data.

You may delete information through your own server administration tools, application settings, or database management procedures, as applicable.

If you have opted into anonymous analytics, you may disable analytics at any time to stop future transmissions.

## 9. Data Security

Nodara is designed to support a privacy-first architecture in which users control their own infrastructure.

However, self-hosting does not automatically guarantee security.

Users are responsible for:

- Securing their server and network.
- Maintaining current software versions.
- Protecting authentication credentials.
- Configuring appropriate access controls.
- Using secure connections.
- Maintaining backups where appropriate.

The Nodara developer cannot guarantee the security of independently operated infrastructure.

The Nodara project may include security improvements and updates as development continues.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Nodara is intended for families and may be used to share location information involving children.

Because Nodara is self-hosted, children's location information is stored and processed on infrastructure controlled by the user or self-hoster.

The Nodara developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the standard self-hosted application.

Parents and guardians are responsible for configuring the application appropriately and ensuring that its use complies with applicable laws and consent requirements.

## 11. International Users

Nodara may be used by individuals and families around the world.

Because users operate their own infrastructure, information may be stored in the country or jurisdiction selected by the user.

The Nodara developer does not control the location of user-operated servers or the legal requirements applicable to those servers.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their deployment complies with applicable privacy and data protection laws.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nodara develops and its functionality evolves.

Changes will be published through the Nodara project website, GitHub repository, or another appropriate project communication channel.

The Last Updated date will be revised when material changes are made.

We encourage users to review this policy periodically.

For questions about this Privacy Policy, the Nodara project, or its privacy practices, please contact the project maintainer through the official GitHub repository.

### Our Privacy Commitment

Nodara is built around a simple principle:

**Your location belongs to you. Your server belongs to you. Your data belongs to you.**

We do not want to know where you are, where your family is, or what you do with Nodara.

The application is designed to give you the benefits of family location sharing without requiring you to trust a centralized company with your family's private information.

Self-host it. Control it. Keep your data yours.
